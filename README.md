Dragon Armoury — Positioning Statement for Regulatory Channels
Use these in CISA / ENISA / standards-body submissions and correspondence.
They translate the project's motive into the constructive frame those bodies
engage with. Keep the resistance framing private; use this frame publicly.

Core paragraph (submission / email body)
The Dragon Armoury Suite is a free, open-source (MIT) collection of tools that
helps smaller manufacturers and developers meet expanding cybersecurity
compliance obligations — particularly the EU Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation
(EU) 2024/2847) and DORA — without enterprise-scale budgets. Built directly
from the primary legal texts, it generates verifiable evidence: binary-level
supply-chain verification, CycloneDX SBOMs, Ed25519 attestation, and incident-
reporting workflows aligned with Article 14 timelines. It is offered as a
community resource: the goal is to lower the cost and complexity of compliance
so that resource-constrained organisations can meet their obligations, and we
welcome feedback on alignment with your SME-support and standardisation work.
The tools assist evidence generation and readiness; they do not replace
conformity-assessment bodies, authorised representatives, or legal advice.

Short version (form field / intro line)
A free, open-source toolkit that helps smaller manufacturers and developers
meet Cyber Resilience Act and DORA obligations by generating verifiable
evidence — SBOMs, binary supply-chain verification, attestation, and
Article 14 reporting workflows.

Channel-specific emphasis
CISA open-source list: lead with free/open-source, community resource,
verifiable evidence, no vendor lock-in, MIT licensed.

ENISA: lead with SME support and Article 14 readiness — align the kit with
ENISA's SME helpdesk and maturity model; offer it as a free resource that helps
SMEs meet the reporting clock; invite feedback on SRP evidence-format alignment.

CEN-CENELEC JTC 24 (CRA harmonised standards): lead with alignment to the
standards-drafting effort — position the evidence-generation approach as a
practical input to harmonised standards; express willingness to contribute
technical detail and reproducible test material.

Rules for every public word
Constructive frame: "make compliance achievable for the little guy."
Not: "thwart/draconian/undemocratic." Same goal, aimed where it lands.
"Verified prototype," never "production-ready."
Assists evidence generation; never replaces conformity assessment or legal
obligations.
Every number carries provenance; no unattributed savings figures.
